The escape plan ↗
  1. Slide a piece any distance in a straight line. Pieces cannot jump.
  2. Sandwich an enemy between two allies to capture it. The throne also affects captures.
  3. Defenders win when the king reaches any edge. Attackers must surround the king.

The king needs four attackers on the throne, three beside it, and a sandwich elsewhere. Only the king can land on the throne. Threefold repetition is a draw; a side with no move loses.
